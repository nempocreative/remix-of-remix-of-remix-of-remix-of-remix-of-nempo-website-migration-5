# Rules for GGS Hair Studio

- GGS Hair Studio (nempocreative/GGS-Hair-Studio-36, visitor app only) is listed on `/client-access` (`src/data/client-access.ts`), rebuilt by `build.sh` here into `public/ggs-hair-studio/` (never hand-edit), served at `/ggs-hair-studio/*` open to all visitors (no access code, at the owner's request); `src/routes/api/ggs/$.ts` answers its original API read-only from the generated starting content, and bookings, accounts and staff tools answer "not connected"; why: the GGS server and database have not been migrated.
- The real GGS logo (owner upload) lives in `brand/` and is placed into the header, sign-in page, footer and tab icon by `patch-logo.mjs` before each build; favicon and touch-icon links use explicit `/ggs-hair-studio/` paths with a cache version; why: rebuilds keep the logo and never load NEMPO's root icons.
- Home menu shortcuts are trimmed and renamed by `patch-menu.mjs` at build time (owner request); why: rebuilds keep the owner's menu.
- Side gutters and top/bottom section spacing are set once per screen size by `patch-layout.mjs`; why: phone, iPad and desktop keep even spacing after rebuilds.
- Price list pages are added by `patch-pricelist.mjs` (women, `/pricelist-wanita`) and `patch-pricelist-pria.mjs` (men, `/pricelist-pria`, dark gold theme reusing the same layout) from their JSON files; each list books through its own WhatsApp helper in `ggs-whatsapp.ts`; why: the barber and the beauty studio take bookings on different numbers.
- Services shown on the home page, services pages and booking form are generated from the two price list JSON files by `pricelist-services.ts` (run from `content.mjs`), tagged `audience` pria/wanita; `patch-beranda.mjs` shows them and routes men's bookings to the barber WhatsApp and the rest to GGS Beauty via `whatsappFor`; why: one source of prices and the two studios take bookings separately.
- The team section (home: one swipeable row per studio, barber then beauty; `/our-team`: swipeable rows on phones) is generated by `patch-team.mjs` from `team-data.ts`, grouped by studio with portraits in `team/` and separate display names and booking roles; why: names can be updated without changing which service or studio gets the request.
- Finger swipe for card rows that the upstream app left grid-only or arrow-only (home banner, hair style cards on phones) is added by `patch-swipe.mjs`; Embla rows already drag; why: every card row must move with a finger on phones and iPads after rebuilds.
- Home "Diskon aktif" torn-ticket cards come from `diskon.json` and are added by `patch-diskon.mjs`; `diskon.ts` (tested) shows only discounts running today in Asia/Jakarta, soonest-ending first (normal price optional: cards without one show only the promo price), and books each on its studio's WhatsApp; the section hides when none run; the row scrolls by itself through `useRowAutoplay` with a duplicate card set (no scroll-snap, pauses on touch/hover/arrows, off under reduced motion); why: the owner edits one file, expired promos disappear by themselves, and the row must keep moving after rebuilds.
- Team rows use duplicate sets and frame-driven forward scrolling in `team-autoplay.ts`, with one nearest-center card enlarged without resizing tracks (pauses on interaction, off under reduced motion); why: seamless loops and alternating emphasis survive rebuilds.
- `optimize-images.sh` shrinks the built photos and icons in place (same names and formats) at the end of `build.sh`; why: the site must stay light without changing any image link.
- Model-only pages and the studio-specific booking form are injected by `patch-booking-models.mjs`; `booking-request.ts` validates requests against the service and team lists before opening the selected studio's WhatsApp, without claiming a reservation was saved; why: the two studios have different staff and contacts.
- The customer account "My GGS" (bottom tab, header account button, sign-in/sign-up pages) is removed by `patch-my-ggs.mjs` at the owner's request; old `/my-ggs`, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up` addresses return to Home; why: accounts are not connected and the owner does not want the menu.
- Service card photos are AI images with Indonesian faces kept in `pricelist-photos/`, copied into the app by `build.sh` and matched to each price-list row by `photo()` in `pricelist-services.ts` (tested); why: every service shows a photo of that service after rebuilds.
- The phone bottom bar's fourth tab "Diskon" and its `/diskon` page (all active discount tickets as a grid, no auto-scroll, empty note when none run) are added by `patch-tab-diskon.mjs` after `patch-my-ggs.mjs`, reusing the home discount component; why: the owner wants discounts one tap away and rebuilds must keep the tab.
- The phone bottom bar's second tab is "Home Service" opening `/home-service`, replacing the upstream "Services" tab (`patch-tab-home-service.mjs`, run after `patch-tab-diskon.mjs`); the services category page stays reachable from the home menu and footer links; why: the owner wants a visit-to-home request one tap away.
- The "si teteh naik motor" nail art video (this project's own asset) plays on the GGS home page (after the quick menu/vouchers, before discounts) and on `/home-service` via `patch-nail-art-video.mjs` (run after `patch-tab-home-service.mjs`), with a tap-to-play fallback under reduced motion and a GGS Beauty WhatsApp button; why: rebuilds keep the video on both pages.
- Every booking button (service cards, price lists, discounts, team, products, Home Service, nail art video) opens one shared order form (`ggs-order-sheet.tsx`, wired by `patch-order-form.mjs` as the last patch); `order-message.ts` (tested) validates it and writes the sectioned WhatsApp message for the right studio, and the `/book` page uses the same message builder; why: every request reaches the studio complete and easy to read, and rebuilds keep the form.
